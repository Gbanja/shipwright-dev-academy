Your path

Fifteen tracks, every lesson hands-on

Each lesson has a sandbox that checks your work as you go: a Git simulator, a Linux and cloud terminal, real code runners for JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Python, SQL and PHP, and an architecture simulator.

Tracks open in order. You can preview any lesson ahead of time; its sandbox opens when you reach it.

Realistic pace
  • Git & GitHub: confident in 1–2 weeks at about 1–2 hours a day.
  • Full path: 15 tracks in 3 phases, about 9–12 months at 2 hours a day.
  • Daily habit: one lesson, one quiz, one real-world rep.
